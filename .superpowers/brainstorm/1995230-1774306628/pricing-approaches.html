Pricing Model Approaches

Three ways to structure Basic & Pro with user bands. All monthly billing. Click the one that feels right.

A

Gentle Ramp

Low entry, gradual scaling. Optimized for adoption.

Band Basic Pro
1–3 users $29/mo $49/mo
4–10 users $59/mo $99/mo
11+ users $59 + $8/user $99 + $12/user

Pros

  • Lowest barrier to entry ($29)
  • Easy upgrade story as team grows
  • Per-user tail scales revenue with big teams

Cons

  • $29 is very low — may attract non-serious signups
  • Bigger gap between band prices needs justification
B

Value Anchor

Slightly higher entry, but Pro feels like a clear deal. The "sweet spot" model.

Band Basic Pro
1–3 users $39/mo $59/mo
4–10 users $69/mo $109/mo
11+ users $69 + $9/user $109 + $14/user

Pros

  • Pro is only $20 more than Basic at each band — easy upsell
  • $39 still feels budget-friendly
  • Better revenue per account than A

Cons

  • Slightly higher barrier than A
  • Small gap may make Basic feel "incomplete"
C

Simple Doubles

Clean, memorable numbers. Each step roughly doubles. Easy to explain on a sales call.

Band Basic Pro
1–3 users $29/mo $59/mo
4–10 users $59/mo $119/mo
11+ users $59 + $10/user $119 + $15/user

Pros

  • Round, memorable numbers
  • Clean doubling pattern is easy to communicate
  • More revenue headroom on Pro

Cons

  • Pro at $59 is 2x Basic — may feel steep for a small shop that just wants Loadboard
  • Bigger gap could slow Pro adoption
