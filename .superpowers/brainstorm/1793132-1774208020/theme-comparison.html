Key Differences

Typography

System fonts → Manrope (headlines) + Inter (body). Tighter tracking on headings.

Border Radius

4px everywhere → 12px for containers, pill-shaped status badges.

Borders & Depth

1px solid borders → tonal layering with ghost borders (15% opacity). Surface hierarchy replaces lines.

Nav & Buttons

Flat blue nav → deep navy (#00174b). Primary buttons get gradient treatment. Secondary buttons lose borders.
